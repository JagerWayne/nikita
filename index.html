Every 250 points = 5 rockets, and points are credits for upgrades (a replayed sector only pays for beating your best). Watch your fuel: an empty tank ends the flight.

      

    


    

      CONTROLS
      

        
STICK / GYRO : Fly, and aim on the pad

        
SPEED LEVER : Throttle, min to max

        
FIRE · LOCK · DET : Launch, lock, blast

        
DECOY : Lure sentries with a noise decoy

        
GUN : Hold to fire the rocket gun

        
AUTO : Full autopilot: scans the map, grabs pickups, shoots sentries, turrets, laser boxes, barrels and grates, hunts the primary targets with the fuze, picks its speed from the room ahead and launches the next rocket by itself. Press again or move the stick to take over

        
HOVER : Brake and hold position (the nose dips as you slow down). Stick up/down = altitude, left/right = rotate, lever = 0 to 4 m/s. To land: hover, then hold the stick down onto a flat floor. To lift off: press HOVER (or raise the lever)

        
DESKTOP : Mouse/WASD, Shift, L, R, Q, H (hover / lift off; hover + S lands), V (auto-discover), X
